# W230 Gear — privacy policy

W230 Gear talks only to your W230 gear indicator over Bluetooth Low Energy.
It collects no personal data, has no accounts, and contains no analytics or
advertising. The WiFi network name and password you enter are sent to the
indicator over the encrypted Bluetooth link and stored only on the
indicator; the app does not keep them. Diagnostic exports are created only
when you use the share button and go wherever you send them. The indicator
itself downloads firmware updates from the project's update server; that
request contains no identifying information.

Questions: https://github.com/digitwolf/w230/issues
