You pull away from a light and the bike stalls: it was in second. At highway speed you go for a seventh gear that does not exist. Through town you lug the little single in sixth. The Kawasaki W230's cluster shows no gear, Kawasaki sells no indicator for it, and universal aftermarket units need a button-pressing calibration ride per gear and splice into your wiring.

This app is the companion to the open-source W230 gear indicator: a matchbox-sized LED matrix that shows N and 1–6, right from the first ride, without cutting a factory wire or writing anything to the ECU. It listens to the diagnostic connector the dealer uses, infers the gear from engine and road speed, and learns your bike's exact ratios as you ride. About 50 USD in parts, an evening on the bench, an hour on the bike.

WHAT YOU GET AT THE HANDLEBAR
• Green N while in neutral, the digit the moment you're in gear.
• Cyan 1–6 following every shift within a beat.
• The right gear at cruise from day one; faster and surer once it has learned your bike (tyres and speedo error included).
• A dim dash when there is no honest answer (stopped, coasting with the clutch in) instead of a guess, and 1 while launching on a slipping clutch.
• All red when the bike is off, so a dead link is never mistaken for neutral.

WHAT THE APP ANSWERS
"Is it right?" Gear: the live digit next to rpm, speed, the neutral switch and where the current ratio sits against the six gear bands. Calibration: factory versus learned bands for each gear, the ratio histogram and its peaks, with a wipe-and-relearn button.

"Why was it wrong?" Troubleshoot: automatic checks written from the bring-up on a real W230 (wiring, power, the neutral diode, learning gates), the display legend, and a one-tap diagnostic export to attach when asking for help. Diagnostics: firmware and partition state, memory, the ride black box (reboots, brownouts, link drops, why samples were or weren't counted) and the event log.

"Do I need the laptop again?" No. Update: give the indicator your 2.4 GHz WiFi once; it then checks for firmware at ignition-on (or when you ask), shows the release notes, installs while you're parked, verifies every image, and rolls back by itself if a new version fails its self-test. Nothing installs unattended unless you choose that.

BEFORE YOU BUILD
Open the connection sheet and tap "Try the demo": every screen fills with data recorded on a real ride, so you can see what you'd get.

PRIVACY
The app talks only to your indicator over Bluetooth. No accounts, no analytics, no data collection. WiFi credentials travel over the encrypted Bluetooth link and are stored only on the indicator.

REQUIRES THE HARDWARE
The indicator is open source: parts list, wiring, firmware and the owner's guide are free at github.com/digitwolf/w230. The gear shown is inferred from rpm and speed; it is a rider aid, not a substitute for knowing what gear you're in.
