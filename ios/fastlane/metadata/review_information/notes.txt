This app is the companion for the open-source W230 gear indicator, a small Bluetooth LE device installed on a Kawasaki W230 motorcycle (hardware and firmware: https://github.com/digitwolf/w230). Without the device the app cannot connect to anything, so it includes a full demo: tap the connection status in the top-right corner, then "Try the demo". Every screen (live gear, diagnostics, calibration, troubleshooting, update) is then populated with data recorded from a real ride, and all controls work against that sample data. Bluetooth is used only to talk to that device; no accounts, no data collection.
