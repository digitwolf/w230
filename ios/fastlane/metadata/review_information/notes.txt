W230 Gear is the companion app for an open-source, build-it-yourself gear indicator for the Kawasaki W230 motorcycle. The indicator is a small Bluetooth LE device (an ESP32 board plugged into the bike's diagnostic connector); parts list, wiring, firmware and the owner's guide are published at https://github.com/digitwolf/w230 (docs/owner-guide.md).

Without the hardware the app has nothing to connect to, so it includes a complete demo: tap the connection status pill in the top-right corner, then "Try the demo". Every screen (Gear, Diagnostics, Calibration, Troubleshoot, Update) is then filled with data recorded on a real ride, and every control (brightness, calibration wipe, WiFi setup, update check, boot policy) works against that sample data. No account or login exists.

Bluetooth is used only to talk to that one device. The app collects no data, has no analytics and no third-party SDKs. The "Update" screen sends WiFi credentials to the indicator over the paired, encrypted Bluetooth link; the indicator then downloads firmware from the project's HTTPS endpoint. The app never downloads or executes code itself.
